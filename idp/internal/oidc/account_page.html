Credits

Available now Credits pay for what apps do for you, such as AI answers or file processing. You are charged only for what is actually used.

Top-ups are one-off payments. A membership adds credits every month and removes the need to top up.

Your credits are used up. Apps that spend for you are paused until you top up.

Apps allowed to spend your credits

Each app spends within a monthly limit you set, for everything it does on your behalf. Lower the limit or stop an app at any time. The change applies within a minute.
